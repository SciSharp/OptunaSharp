Inside this experiment +

Problem: three-objective DTLZ2, 12 decision variables in [0, 1]. Its analytic Pareto surface is the positive octant of a sphere of radius 1. The thin surface in raw objective space is that target.

Evolution: NSGA-III selection, simulated binary crossover and polynomial mutation. Earlier non-dominated fronts survive first. A splitting front is reduced using reference-direction niches.

Normalization: ideal-point translation and intercept scaling are reconstructed from the server’s selection diagnostics.

Reading the metrics: proximity to the sphere and occupied reference directions describe different aspects of progress. A small distance alone does not establish a well-distributed front. Occupancy uses the generation’s normalized reference associations.
